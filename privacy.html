Moonth

Privacy Policy

Effective November 2, 2026

Moonth helps parents and caregivers see a baby’s age and milestones in the app and in iOS widgets. We designed Moonth to keep personal family information on the user’s device whenever possible.

Information stored on your device

Moonth stores the information you enter—such as a baby’s display name, date of birth, optional birth time, widget preferences, age format, theme selection, and notification preferences—locally on your device and in the app’s shared iOS container used by its widgets. Moonth does not require an account and does not upload this family information to a Moonth-operated server.

Purchases

When you purchase or restore Moonth Pro, Apple processes the transaction. RevenueCat helps the app retrieve product information and verify purchase entitlement. Apple and RevenueCat may process transaction identifiers, an app user identifier, product and entitlement information, and limited device or app metadata needed to provide and secure purchases. Moonth does not receive your full payment-card details. Their handling of this information is described in Apple’s Privacy Policy and RevenueCat’s Privacy Policy.

Notifications

If you grant notification permission, Moonth can schedule milestone reminders locally through iOS. Notification permission is optional and can be changed at any time in iOS Settings.

Analytics, advertising, and tracking

Moonth does not include third-party advertising, cross-app tracking, or a third-party analytics service. Limited onboarding and feature events used by the app are stored locally on the device.

Children’s privacy

Moonth is intended for parents and caregivers. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children. Information about a child that a parent or caregiver enters remains on the device as described above.

Data retention and deletion

You can edit or delete baby profiles in Moonth. Removing the app removes its local app data according to iOS behavior. Purchase records may continue to be retained by Apple and RevenueCat as required to provide purchase history, restoration, fraud prevention, accounting, or legal compliance.

Your choices

You may decline notification permission, avoid entering optional information, edit or delete profiles, and use Apple’s purchase-management tools. Requests concerning Apple or RevenueCat data may also be directed to those providers under their respective privacy policies.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when Moonth’s features or legal obligations change. The effective date above identifies the current version.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact the developer through Moonth’s App Store support page.
