Set up in under a minute

Three steps. Following Apple's own guide.

Moonth walks you through it inside the app, with a live preview of your baby's name and age. Here's the short version.

🔒

On the Lock Screen

  1. Touch and hold the Lock Screen until Customize appears, then tap Customize → Lock Screen.
  2. Tap the widget area under the clock (Add Widgets).
  3. Choose Moonth, pick rectangular, circular or inline, then tap Done.
📱

On the Home Screen

  1. Touch and hold an empty spot until the app icons start to jiggle.
  2. Tap Edit → Add Widget (or +) in the top-left corner.
  3. Find Moonth, choose Small, Medium or Large, and tap Add Widget.

Questions

Good to know

Does the widget update on its own?

Yes. The widget recalculates the age from the birth date every time iOS refreshes it, so it rolls over to the next day — and the next month — without you opening the app.

Can I use it only on the Lock Screen?

Absolutely. Lock Screen and Home Screen widgets are independent. Use one, the other, or both.

How is the age calculated?

Moonth uses real calendar months. If the birth day doesn't exist in a shorter month (like the 31st), the month-iversary is clamped to the last day of that month. Leap days are handled too.

Which iPhones are supported?

Any iPhone running iOS 16 or later. Lock Screen widgets need iOS 16+, StandBy needs iOS 17+.

Is Pro a subscription?

No. Moonth Pro is a single, one-time purchase handled by Apple. You can restore it on any of your devices.

Where is my data stored?

Only on your iPhone, inside the app and its widget container. Deleting the app deletes the data. Read the full Privacy Policy.
